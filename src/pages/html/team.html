1. What does SYNQENTRA do?

Web and mobile development, AI and automation, CRM, WhatsApp solutions, conversational AI, and SEO and digital growth — delivered by one team so your tools work together.

2. How can AI help my business?

AI assistants and monitored workflows take over repetitive work, and chatbots answer, qualify and route enquiries 24/7. AI outputs are reviewed by a person.

Yes. Every project is scoped to your goals, from business websites and e-commerce to custom web and mobile apps.

It depends on scope. We confirm the timeline in a written proposal before work begins.

5. Who do you work with?

Startups, SMEs and growing businesses. We are based in India and deliver remotely for clients worldwide.

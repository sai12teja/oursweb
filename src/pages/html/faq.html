Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to the questions we hear most about our services, how projects run, pricing and support.

1. What services does SYNQENTRA offer?

Web and mobile development, AI and automation, CRM, WhatsApp solutions, conversational AI, and SEO and digital growth — delivered by one team so your tools work together.

2. Who do you work with?

Startups, SMEs and growing businesses. We are based in India and deliver remotely for clients worldwide.

3. How much does a project cost?

Pricing is by proposal. We scope the work to your goals and confirm deliverables, timeline and price in writing before we start.

4. How do you run a project?

In three stages: discover and plan, design and build, then launch and support. You get a clear scope, timeline and success criteria up front.

5. Do you connect to our existing tools?

Yes. We connect websites, apps, CRM and messaging so your business runs as one system, using licensed software and approved APIs.

1. Where are you based?

We are based in India and deliver remotely for clients worldwide.

2. How can technology help my business grow?

By cutting repetitive work with AI assistants and monitored workflows, keeping leads and follow-ups in one place, and answering customer enquiries around the clock with chatbots.

3. Do you build custom solutions?

Yes. Every project is scoped to your goals, from business websites and e-commerce to custom web and mobile apps.

4. How long does a project take?

It depends on scope. We confirm the timeline in a written proposal before work begins.

5. Do you offer ongoing support?

Yes. After launch we hand over with documentation, then support and improve the product as you grow. A retainer covers ongoing maintenance and agreed support hours.
